Who We Are

A Movement
Built on Belief

Queens Of Change Foundation was born from a simple belief: every woman carries within her the power to transform her world. We are a non-profit organisation dedicated to the education, health, and leadership of women and girls across underserved communities.

We don't just offer programmes — we build lasting sisterhood. Through mentorship, skill-building workshops, mental health support, and advocacy, we walk with women from where they are to where they dream to be.

When a woman rises, her family rises. When her family rises, her community rises. That is the Queens Of Change effect.

What We Do

Our Four Pillars
of Change

Every programme we run sits on one of these four foundations.

📚

Education

Scholarships, literacy drives, and digital skills training that open doors for girls who never thought higher learning was meant for them.

💼

Economic Power

Micro-grants, entrepreneurship bootcamps, and career mentoring that turn ambition into income and independence.

🩺

Health & Wellbeing

Reproductive health awareness, mental wellness circles, and nutrition programmes that affirm every woman's right to thrive.

👑

Leadership

Cultivating the next generation of women leaders through public speaking, policy engagement, and community governance.
