About Us

Queens Of Change Foundation is a non-profit organisation working to empower women across India. We believe every woman deserves access to education, equal rights, and the chance to lead. Since 2016, we have supported over 12,000 women in 38 communities through scholarships, skill training, and mentorship programmes.

Our Programs

📚 Crown Scholars

Full scholarships for underprivileged girls entering higher education. Over 800 scholars enrolled this year.

💼 Rise & Lead

A 6-month leadership programme connecting women entrepreneurs with mentors and seed funding.

💻 Digital Divas

Coding bootcamps and digital literacy workshops helping women enter the tech industry.
